## 2. Search-result and competitor evidence

### Expiry / MFG topic

- The site's English expiry article is indexed in public search results with a direct EXP/MFG title and practical examples. Search-result extraction shows a date decoder and clear explanation of MFG versus EXP.
- The site's Bangla article targets the exact Bangla phrase “ওষুধের মেয়াদ শেষ হওয়ার তারিখ” and has a Bangladesh-specific opportunity in GSC.
- ePharma Bangladesh has a recent article on spotting fake medicine, batch numbers, expiry and storage. Its angle is broader counterfeit/pack inspection and safe purchasing, rather than a focused EXP/MFG decoder.
- Tukri's Bangladesh pharmacy content is mainly inventory operations and FEFO, not a patient-facing explanation of printed EXP/MFG.
- Talhaq has an expiry calculator and a use-after-opening explanation, but the source is not a Bangladesh regulator and its month/year rule should not replace Bangladesh-specific regulatory guidance.

**Differentiation to preserve:** A Bangladesh-first, patient-readable guide that explains EXP, MFG, batch/lot number, month-year interpretation, storage and the difference between printed expiry and shorter in-use periods. The article already contains substantial material; improve the opening answer, search snippet and official-source prominence rather than adding another generic expiry article.

### QA vs QC topic

- The current Bangla QA/QC page is discoverable in public search and presents a clear early distinction: QA is the planned system that helps prevent quality failures; QC covers sampling, specifications, testing and evaluation.
- Bangladesh's Essential Drugs Company Limited provides a local institutional explanation of QA and QC laboratory functions, including sampling/testing, validation, calibration and data integrity.
- A 2024 research paper discusses QA/QC's role in Bangladesh's pharmaceutical industry and global standards.

**Differentiation to preserve:** A practical pharmaceutical-workflow explanation, with QA system ownership and QC testing/evaluation clearly separated, then linked through deviation, OOS, CAPA, change control, batch review and GMP. Avoid implying QA and QC are competitors or that one can replace the other.

### API topic

- The current Bangla API page already uses a concise question-led title: “API কী? ওষুধ তৈরিতে Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient”.
- Search Console shows a small number of Bangladesh impressions, but broader query examples include “API full form in pharma” and “API meaning pharma”.
- The Bangladesh pharma-industry article already explains API versus finished pharmaceutical product and manufacturing stages.

**Differentiation to preserve:** Answer “API = Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient” immediately, distinguish API from excipients and finished pharmaceutical product (FPP), and explain quality controls and GMP. Avoid outdated or undated claims about Bangladesh's number of API producers, self-sufficiency or import dependence.

## 3. Authoritative source checks

- **DGDA Bangladesh labelling guideline:** The official guideline says that an expiry date printed with only month and year means the last day of that month. It also calls for applicable shelf-life information after reconstitution, dilution or first opening. This is the strongest local source for the article's Bangladesh-specific explanation. See the official guideline, page 11: https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-dgda/2024/12/f7a2b793f8a6460db4fe809a4cdba158.pdf
- **Bangladesh EDCL QA page:** Local institutional context for QA, QC laboratory testing, validation, calibration and data integrity: https://edcl.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dd27933eb65569e13b5c
- **FDA ICH Q7 guidance:** Useful international GMP reference for API manufacturing; do not present it as Bangladesh law.
- **WHO terminology:** Use WHO definitions to distinguish API and finished pharmaceutical product. Check the exact current source edition before quoting a definition.
