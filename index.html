Pharmacist & Brand Manager

Sayedul Abrar is a Product Executive in the Product Management Department at Opsonin Pharma Ltd., managing the pharmaceutical brand Finix® and contributing to its market development and strategy.

As a Pharmacy alumnus of Daffodil International University, he is dedicated to patient-centred pharmaceutical solutions through strategic brand development, innovation and collaboration. His professional focus includes antiulcerant and gastro portfolio management, Finix® and Finix MUPS® brand development, pharmaceutical marketing, brand strategy, campaign planning and prescription data analysis.
