## Website purpose

SayedulAbrar.site is a personal professional website and pharmaceutical knowledge platform dedicated to accurate, accessible, evidence-based educational content about medicines, pharmaceutical science, healthcare and the pharmaceutical industry.

The site should establish Sayedul Abrar's professional identity and expertise in pharmaceutical science, product management, pharmaceutical marketing and medicine education. It must represent professional experience accurately and must not imply clinical credentials, prescribing authority, or responsibilities that have not been documented.

## Editorial voice and standards

- Evidence-based and medically responsible.
- Professional, clear, human-sounding and conversational.
- Explain scientific concepts in accessible language without losing accuracy.
- Include practical examples where they genuinely help.
- Avoid unsupported medical claims and misleading treatment recommendations.
- Use Bangladesh-specific context when relevant and support local claims with appropriate local sources.
- Prefer British English spelling while keeping wording understandable internationally.
- Avoid inflated, promotional, robotic or repetitive language.
- Preserve the author's authentic experience, examples and professional perspective.
- Do not turn educational content into product promotion.

## Medical and scientific accuracy

Prefer primary and authoritative sources, including peer-reviewed research, systematic reviews, WHO, FDA, EMA, NHS, recognised pharmacopoeias, national regulators and official product information. Match each source to the exact claim, formulation, population, route, dose, jurisdiction and publication date.

Distinguish established evidence, emerging evidence, clinical practice and opinion. Never invent citations, studies, statistics, dates, approval status, prices, dosage instructions or regulatory claims. Flag unsupported or potentially harmful claims with [SOURCE NEEDED]. Do not present general educational content as individual medical advice. For Bangladesh-specific regulatory, availability or practice claims, seek Bangladesh-relevant authoritative evidence where possible.

## SEO and helpful-content standards

- Answer the reader's main question early.
- Use the primary keyword naturally; do not force keyword density or exact-match repetition.
- Cover related entities and subtopics only when they help the reader.
- Use descriptive, logically ordered headings.
- Add FAQs only when they answer genuine reader questions not already adequately addressed.
- Write accurate title tags, meta descriptions and image alt text.
- Identify relevant internal-link opportunities using existing live URLs.
- Do not create thin, duplicate or near-duplicate articles to target keyword variations.
- Do not claim that E-E-A-T is a measurable Google ranking score; use it as a quality and trust framework.
