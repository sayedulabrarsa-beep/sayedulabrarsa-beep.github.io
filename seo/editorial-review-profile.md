## Website positioning

SayedulAbrar.site is a personal professional website and pharmaceutical knowledge platform. It publishes educational material about medicines, pharmaceutical science, pharmacology, clinical pharmacy, medicine safety, healthcare awareness, pharmaceutical marketing and the pharmaceutical industry.

The site should demonstrate the author's professional knowledge and practical perspective without implying clinical credentials, prescribing authority, or experience that has not been explicitly documented.

## Intended readers

- Pharmacy students, graduates and pharmacists
- Healthcare professionals
- Pharmaceutical industry and product-management professionals
- Medical and pharmaceutical representatives
- General readers seeking understandable, reliable medicine information

## Editorial voice

- Professional, clear, evidence-led and approachable
- Human and direct; avoid inflated, promotional or robotic language
- Explain technical terms when first introduced
- Prefer practical examples and useful steps over generic commentary
- Respect the author's real experience, examples and point of view
- Avoid first-person claims unless supported by the draft or supplied context
- Do not turn educational content into product promotion

## Medical and scientific accuracy

- Treat medicine-related content as health information requiring careful verification.
- Prefer primary and authoritative sources: peer-reviewed research, systematic reviews, WHO, FDA, EMA, NHS, recognised pharmacopoeias, national regulators and official product information.
- Match every source to the exact claim, formulation, population, route, dose, jurisdiction and publication date.
- Distinguish established evidence, emerging evidence, clinical practice and opinion.
- Never invent citations, studies, statistics, dates, approval status, prices, dosage instructions or regulatory claims.
- Flag unsupported or potentially harmful claims. Use [SOURCE NEEDED] where evidence is required.
- Do not present general educational content as individual medical advice.
- For Bangladesh-specific regulatory, availability or practice claims, seek Bangladesh-relevant authoritative evidence where possible.

## SEO and helpful-content standards

- Satisfy the search intent early, preferably in the opening paragraph or a concise answer block.
- Use the primary keyword naturally; do not force density or exact-match repetition.
- Cover relevant entities and subtopics only when they help the reader.
- Make headings descriptive and logically ordered.
- Add FAQs only when they answer genuine reader questions not already adequately answered.
- Use concise, accurate title tags, meta descriptions, image alt text and internal-link suggestions when the article context supports them.
- Do not promise rankings, traffic or indexing outcomes.
- Prioritise usefulness, originality, trust and clarity over keyword counts.

## Website implementation constraints

- The site is a static GitHub Pages website. Blog source content is stored as JSON in `_src/content/blog/<slug>.json`; generated pages and site assets are built from repository source files.
- Do not edit generated pages as a substitute for editing the source content.
- Do not change the homepage, shared navigation, CSS, JavaScript, sitemap, robots.txt, URL slugs, images, metadata or any published article during an editorial review unless specifically requested and approved.
- Preserve existing URLs, article structure, images, internal links, language variants and author attribution unless a proposed change is explicitly approved.
- Every new article must follow the site's current shared navigation standard documented in `_src/README.md`.

## Approval policy

**Review-only by default.** Produce the scorecard, ranked fixes, fact-check list, complete corrected draft and change log. Do not commit editorial changes to `main`, publish, merge a pull request, or alter live pages without the author's explicit approval. When repository changes are requested, prepare them on a separate branch and open a pull request for review.
