## Search intent interpretation
The SERP is fragmented into four intents:
1. **Informational/professional learning:** understand PMD, brand lifecycle and decision-making.
2. **Career/training:** role expectations, skills, interview preparation and courses.
3. **Employment:** responsibilities, qualifications and vacancies.
4. **Academic/research:** evidence about positioning, promotional investment and Bangladesh market practice.

The target page should primarily satisfy informational/professional learning. It can answer career questions briefly but should not become a job board or compete with a future pharmacy-career guide.

The English article appeared in the search snapshot for the exact target phrase. Treat this as an early discovery signal only—not a stable ranking, traffic or conversion claim.

## Differentiation and content gaps
- A coherent PMD operating model instead of a standalone interview, course syllabus, academic paper or job description.
- KPI definitions with source, period, denominator and limitations; prescription, sales and market share must not be treated as interchangeable.
- Monthly execution governance across medical, regulatory, supply, sales, finance and marketing.
- Ethical, evidence-based HCP engagement that rejects inducements and intrusive personal profiling.
- Bangladesh context grounded in official DGDA/WHO sources rather than unsupported competitor claims.
- A practical one-page brand-plan checklist.
