01 / ABOUT ABRAR

Science at heart.
Strategy in action.

I’m a pharmacy graduate of Daffodil International University and a Product Executive in the Product Management Department at Opsonin Pharma Ltd.

My work brings together pharmaceutical knowledge, brand development, campaign planning and prescription-led market understanding. I enjoy turning complex information into clear communication, and translating insights into practical plans that support brand growth.

This website is my corner of the internet to document what I do, what I learn and the experiences that have shaped me along the way.

02 / MY PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

From learning the science
to shaping the strategy.

My early experience introduced me to community pharmacy and the systems behind pharmaceutical manufacturing, quality and product development. These foundations helped me understand how medicines move from production to the people who rely on them.

At Navana Pharmaceuticals PLC, I worked as a Brand Executive in Strategic Brand Management, supporting NSAIDs and Vitamins & Minerals portfolios, including Malifa, Megamag and Loxodol.

Since January 2025, I’ve worked as a Product Executive at Opsonin Pharma Ltd., contributing to the Gastro portfolio and brand development for Finix®, Finix MUPS and Finix HP.
