05 / HOW I GOT HERE

Every chapter
added a new perspective.

At Daffodil International University, studying pharmacy gave me a foundation in medicines and healthcare. Student organisations, ambassador roles and volunteer activities helped me develop communication, teamwork and event-planning skills beyond the classroom.

My early industry exposure introduced me to pharmaceutical production, quality functions and community pharmacy. At Navana Pharmaceuticals, I supported brand development across NSAIDs and vitamins and minerals. Today, at Opsonin Pharma Ltd., I work in Product Management, contributing to the Finix® portfolio and its brand strategy.

Each experience has taught me to stay curious, work collaboratively and connect the details with the bigger picture.
