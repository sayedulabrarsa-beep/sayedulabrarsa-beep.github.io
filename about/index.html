02 / MY PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

From learning science
to planning what comes next.

My early roles taught me about community pharmacy, how medicines are made and how their quality is checked. I learned how medicines reach the people who need them.

At Navana Pharmaceuticals PLC, I worked as a Brand Executive in Strategic Brand Management. I worked with NSAID and Vitamins & Minerals brands, including Malifa, Megamag and Loxodol.

I joined Opsonin Pharma Ltd. as a Product Executive in January 2025. I work with the Gastro team on Finix®, Finix MUPS and Finix HP.
