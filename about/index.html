About Sayedul Abrar

Pharmacist, Product Executive and brand strategist focused on patient-centred pharmaceutical solutions.

Summary

Sayedul Abrar currently serves as a Product Executive in the Product Management Department at Opsonin Pharma Ltd., managing the pharmaceutical brand Finix® and contributing to its market development and strategy.

He graduated with a Bachelor of Pharmacy from Daffodil International University and holds certifications in drug development and decision-making. He is dedicated to delivering impactful, patient-centred pharmaceutical solutions through strategic brand development and a focus on innovation and collaboration.

His experience spans Gastro, NSAIDs, and vitamin and mineral portfolios, with key brands including Finix and Malifa. He analyses Rx data and turns the findings into targeted marketing strategies that support brand growth and market penetration.

Recognised at the Annual PMD Iconic Award 2025 for both Best Productive and Best Innovative performance.
