Finix HP

Opsonin Pharma Ltd.

Product overview

Finix HP is a combination product containing a proton pump inhibitor and two antibiotics.

Manufacturer-listed indications

  • The manufacturer lists Finix HP for eradication of H. pylori in active chronic gastric and duodenal ulcer disease.

Mode of action

Rabeprazole suppresses gastric acid secretion; amoxicillin inhibits bacterial cell-wall synthesis; clarithromycin inhibits bacterial protein synthesis.

Important information

Antibiotic combination therapy must be used only under a qualified prescriber's direction. Consult the current official product insert for full approved prescribing information.
