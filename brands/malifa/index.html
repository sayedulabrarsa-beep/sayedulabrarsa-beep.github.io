Malifa 30

Navana Pharmaceuticals PLC

Product overview

Malifa 30 is an oral ferric maltol capsule listed by Navana Pharmaceuticals for treatment of iron deficiency in adults.

Manufacturer-listed indications

  • Treatment of iron deficiency in adults, according to the manufacturer's product information.

Mode of action

Ferric maltol provides iron for uptake across the intestinal wall and transfer to iron-storage and transport proteins.

Important information

This page is a summary of public manufacturer information, not medical advice. Consult the current official label and a qualified healthcare professional for clinical decisions.
