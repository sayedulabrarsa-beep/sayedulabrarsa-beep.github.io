Finix MUPS 20

Finix MUPS 20 is a rabeprazole sodium 20 mg proton pump inhibitor (PPI) presented as an enteric-coated Multiple-Unit Pellet System (MUPS) tablet. Product overview

Finix MUPS 20 is a rabeprazole sodium 20 mg proton pump inhibitor (PPI) presented as an enteric-coated Multiple-Unit Pellet System (MUPS) tablet. MUPS is a multiparticulate formulation approach: the dose is carried in multiple coated units rather than one single monolithic core. The enteric protection is designed to protect the acid-sensitive drug during gastric transit and allow release beyond the stomach, according to the product's formulation design.

Composition & dosage forms

  • 20 mg enteric-coated MUPS tablet
  • Each tablet contains Rabeprazole Sodium BP 20 mg, as stated in the manufacturer insert
  • Manufacturer insert describes Alu-Alu blister within Alu-Alu sachet packaging

Manufacturer-listed indications

  • Healing of erosive or ulcerative gastro-oesophageal reflux disease (GERD)
  • Maintenance of healing of erosive or ulcerative GERD
  • Treatment of symptomatic GERD
  • Healing of gastric and duodenal ulcers
  • Long-term treatment of pathological hypersecretory conditions, including Zollinger–Ellison syndrome
  • In combination with amoxicillin and clarithromycin for Helicobacter pylori eradication, when prescribed as an appropriate regimen

How it works

Rabeprazole inhibits the gastric H⁺/K⁺-ATPase (proton pump) in gastric parietal cells, reducing the final step of gastric acid secretion. Reduced acid exposure can support healing in specified acid-related conditions.

Important clinical considerations

A PPI is not a universal treatment for every cause of abdominal pain, gas, bloating or indigestion. Persistent symptoms, difficulty swallowing, vomiting blood, black stools, unexplained weight loss, recurrent vomiting or severe chest/abdominal pain require prompt medical assessment. The prescriber should review other medicines, relevant medical history and duration of treatment.

Frequently asked questions

What does MUPS mean?

MUPS means Multiple-Unit Pellet System. It is a multiparticulate tablet formulation containing multiple coated units.

What is the active ingredient in Finix MUPS 20?

The manufacturer insert states that each Finix MUPS 20 mg tablet contains Rabeprazole Sodium BP 20 mg.

Is Finix MUPS a PPI?

Yes. Rabeprazole is a proton pump inhibitor that reduces gastric acid secretion by inhibiting the gastric H⁺/K⁺-ATPase proton pump in gastric parietal cells.

Is Finix MUPS the fastest or number-one PPI in Bangladesh?

This page does not make an independently verified fastest-acting or national ranking claim. Comparative claims require appropriate clinical evidence and a clearly defined comparison.

Does Finix MUPS treat all types of gas?

No PPI treats every cause of gas or bloating. Rabeprazole reduces gastric acid and is used for specific acid-related conditions listed in the product information. A clinician should assess persistent or severe symptoms.
