Product overview

Finix MUPS 20 is a rabeprazole sodium antiulcerant in the proton pump inhibitor class. MUPS means Multiple-Unit Pellet System.

Manufacturer-listed indications

  • Healing of erosive or ulcerative gastro-esophageal reflux disease (GERD)
  • Maintenance of healing of erosive or ulcerative GERD
  • Treatment of symptomatic GERD
  • Healing of gastric and duodenal ulcers
  • Long-term treatment of pathological hypersecretory conditions, including Zollinger–Ellison syndrome
  • In combination with amoxicillin and clarithromycin to eradicate Helicobacter pylori

Mode of action

Rabeprazole suppresses gastric acid secretion by inhibiting the gastric H+/K+-ATPase enzyme at the secretory surface of gastric parietal cells.
