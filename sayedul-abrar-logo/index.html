Sayedul Abrar Logo

This is the official personal brand monogram used to identify Sayedul Abrar (Abrar), Pharmacist and Product Executive in Bangladesh.

What the SA monogram stands for

The two intertwined letters are the initials of Sayedul Abrar. The gold-on-black colours are used across this website, presentation slides and personal brand materials so people can recognise the same person in every place.

Who is Sayedul Abrar?

Sayedul Abrar, known to most people simply as Abrar, is a pharmacist and Product Executive in the Product Management Department of Opsonin Pharma Ltd. in Dhaka, Bangladesh. He studied pharmacy at Daffodil International University and works on pharmaceutical brand strategy, product launches and doctor engagement. He also writes the Pharma Blog, which explains everyday medicine topics in English and Bangla.

Name spellings

Because the name is transliterated from Bangla and Arabic, it appears in several spellings: Sayyidul Abrar, Sayyedul Abrar, Syedul Abrar and Saidul Abrar. In Bangla it is written সাইয়্যেদুল আবরার. Searches for “Abrar pharmacist” or “pharmacist Abrar Bangladesh” refer to the same person.
